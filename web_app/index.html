AI-Driven Targeted Protein Degradation (TPD) Lead Generation for Acute Myeloid Leukemia

Targeted Protein Degradation represents an event-driven paradigm where a heterobifunctional PROTAC catalyzes the proximity-induced polyubiquitination and 26S proteasomal lysis of oncogenic drivers. This platform implements an end-to-end computational pipeline optimizing chemical linkers to stabilize the BRD4(BD1) : PROTAC : Cereblon ternary complex, maximizing thermodynamic cooperativity (α > 1) and suppressing non-productive hook effects.
